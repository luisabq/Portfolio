About

I'm a game design student who works across technical art, production, and UI/UX. I like making things look right and run smoothly, and I like helping a team get there together.

What I do

  • Technical art: 3D modeling, UV mapping, retopology, PBR texturing, procedural materials, and shader development.
  • Production: milestone planning, task tracking, team coordination, and QA testing.
  • UI/UX design: interface layouts and player-facing feedback built for clarity and usability.

Tools

Maya, ZBrush, Substance Designer, Substance Painter, Unreal Engine 5, Unity, Git, ClickUp. I program in C#, C/C++, and JavaScript.

Background

I study Digital Media (Game Design track) at the University of Central Florida, graduating in May 2027. I'm Head of Technology for BRASA at UCF, where I lead the team that maintains the organization's website and tools. My retail and front-desk work has taught me to listen to people, solve problems quickly, and communicate clearly, and I bring that to every team.

I speak English and Portuguese fluently, and Spanish at an intermediate level.
